02 — The Channel

Heavy
machines.
Real dirt.

DIRT MONEY is a Western Australian content brand built around heavy earthmoving, broadacre farming, and machinery pushed to the limit. Real operators. Real equipment. No studio sets.

The audience found us in week one because they could immediately tell the difference between a brand that lives this life and one that's pretending to. 10 million views. Zero advertising dollars.

Based in the WA Wheatbelt. Filmed on working farms, job sites, and the roads between them.

THE BUILD SERIES

The hero project: a John Deere 9750 STS combine stripped back and rebuilt into something that has no right to move the way it does. Eight to ten episodes. Filmed on the Sunnydale farm in Western Australia.

Long-form integration opportunity. Highest-attention format on the channel.

04 — Content Formats
Format 01
Build Series
Long-form episodic content following a full machine build. Deep integration, brand presence across multiple episodes. The highest-attention format on the channel. YouTube + Reels cuts.
Format 02
Machine Tests
Real-world capability tests. Pushing equipment to find the limits. Product placement and integration opportunities for tools, lubricants, and equipment brands.
Format 03
Day-in-the-Dirt
Raw, unscripted content from the farm and job site. High authenticity, strong engagement. Perfect for workwear, tools, and ag brands wanting genuine placement.
Format 04
Carnage Cuts
Short-form destruction content. Things breaking, being pushed, doing what they're not supposed to do. Viral-optimised. Reel-first format. High reach, fast.
