01 — About
Heavy
machines.
Real dirt.

DIRT MONEY is a Western Australian content brand built around heavy earthmoving, broadacre farming, and machinery that gets pushed to the limit. Real operators. Real equipment. No studio sets.

Founded by Jacob Turner — qualified heavy diesel mechanic and broadacre farmer — and Cam, with years of earthmoving experience between them. Everything you see is lived, not performed.

The audience followed because they recognised the authenticity immediately. 3 million views in the first week, zero paid media.

The Build Series

The hero project: a John Deere 9750 STS combine stripped back and rebuilt into something that has no right to move the way it does. Eight to ten episodes. Filmed on the Sunnydale farm in Western Australia.

The finale? A river crossing. Filmed after the series drops.

This is the content that launched the channel. Every brand on this build will be in front of an audience that actually understands what they're watching.

03 — Content Formats

Four ways
in.

01
Build Series
Long-form episodic content following a full machine build. Deep integration, brand presence across multiple episodes. The highest-attention format on the channel. YouTube + Reels cuts.
02
Machine Tests
Real-world capability tests. Pushing equipment to find the limits. Product placement and integration opportunities for tools, lubricants, and equipment brands.
03
Day-in-the-Dirt
Raw, unscripted content from the farm and job site. High authenticity, strong engagement. Perfect for workwear, tools, and ag brands wanting a genuine placement.
04
Carnage Cuts
Short-form destruction content. Things breaking, being pushed, or doing what they're not supposed to do. Viral-optimised. Reel-first format. High reach, fast.
